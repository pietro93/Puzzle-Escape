## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Game Progress**: We store your game progress locally on your device.
- **Device Settings**: We store your preferences (such as sound settings) locally on your device.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: We collect information about your device, including model, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
- **Usage Data**: We collect information about how you use the App, including game progress, time spent playing, and feature usage.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Improve the App's performance and features
- Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience

## Data Storage and Security

All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store this data on our servers.

## Third-Party Services

## Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
